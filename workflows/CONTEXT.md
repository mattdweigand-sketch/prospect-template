# Optional subscription workflows

These independent workflows require a subscription business model and reviewed warehouse mappings. They are disabled in the starter configuration.

| Workflow | Job |
|---|---|
| `adoption/CONTEXT.md` | Read permitted account-level adoption context |
| `arr-growth/CONTEXT.md` | Propose fixed-template drafts for eligible USD-normalized ARR growth |

Read the selected contract and procedure, then only their named references. `../AGENTS.md` owns skill routing. These workflows do not form a sequence and do not run automatically after the numbered stages.

Review and operational state follow `../CONTEXT.md`; no customer records are stored here.
