# Signal-based outreach draft

Prepare one cold email from one explicitly handed-off qualified public signal.
Create one **unsent** mail draft only after exact approval.

## Load / Skip

- Working: output/{run-id}/request.md; the exact named scan run's reviewed bundle, source receipt/text and review reference; an explicitly supplied adoption bundle only when needed.
- Reference: workflows/run.md, _shared/rules.md; policy.json identity, outreach, email_voice and adoption only if used; the selected taxonomy entry; relevant approved claims.json rows and held list; icp.md verticals/persona_cares; adapters.md CRM contact/activity, mail sent-read/draft and optional verified enrichment.
- Skip: fresh signal discovery, private warehouse queries, upstream knowledge sources, unrelated accounts and prior runs. No bundle means stop; never reconstruct one from memory, a snippet or a summary.

## Process

1. Check the handoff has gate=evidence_gate, quote, source_url, signal_type, governing published_date, date_basis, account_domain, declared aliases and timezone-aware checked_at. The selected taxonomy entry must have source web; an adoption signal cannot replace the public signal. Re-fetch and re-qualify the same page if the elapsed check age exceeds outreach.bundle_max_age_hours; preserve actual fetch time. Future timestamps/dates and date-only read times block. Confirm the CRM entity, ownership route, alias review and absence of open deals/warm engagement before proceeding.
2. An adoption sentence additionally requires a privacy-clean reviewed adoption bundle for this account from the named run, confirmed query success, current complete data date and fresh checked_at. Use only the category's exact approved_statements text. No current bundle means omit the sentence; none_found/unknown authorizes no claim. Do not perform private queries here.
3. Prefer the person named in the signal. Resolve a real name/title and verified work address from outreach.recipient_sources in order: existing CRM contact, a prior mail thread with that person, then verified enrichment. Missing verification requires the user to supply the address; never guess. Require the account domain or an established subdomain. Map the title to an icp.md persona. Match target_titles, or a named quoted executive to evidence_subject; otherwise obtain and record the user's specific title_override reason.
4. Read complete CRM tasks and events on both account and contact, and mail sent messages to the recipient, covering at least the full suppression window. Keep all rows unfiltered in the gate packet, with pagination/completion receipt references. Preserve native task statuses and use the reviewed outreach.task_status_map to classify them; unknown statuses stop the gate. Integration-owner exclusions from scanning do **not** apply here. Completed tasks in configured email/call subtypes, events or sent mail within the window suppress outreach. Failed/incomplete reads are not empty activity.
5. Select by signal first. Candidates are the signal's bound claim IDs plus rows whose verticals are not [all] and include the account's vertical. Read the quote and the taxonomy entry's creates_work, then choose the candidate whose claim most directly answers them. Persona never narrows or ranks the candidates; it shapes the wording and closing question. If no candidate answers, choose the closest defensible approved claim in the library. Name the second-best claim as runner-up, or say none when no other defensible claim exists. Classify relevance: direct when the claim answers the quote's own words and the work the signal creates; exploratory when it answers creates_work but the quote does not name that work (use a tentative question); best_guess when no bound row answers the quote or work. A selection outside the binding, vertical or persona is also best_guess; name each gap. Every selected claim must be status approved, not held, and have a current unit stamp; the signal entry and persona_cares also need current stamps. Use kind, claim and limit to bound meaning. Binding/vertical/persona gaps are disclosed flags; fit_mode=warn permits exact human review and fit_mode=block holds them. A vertical fit alone does not create a signal binding.
6. Copy the selected claim ID, evidence and persona into the packet. Add claim.pick_reason: one sentence tying the quote's words to the selected claim. The gate requires a nonblank string; the human meaning review determines whether that explanation is supported. The evidence verifies the row and stays out of the email. Product assertions come only from the approved claim/track within its limit. A reported example is not a general guarantee; inference and evaluation advice must remain visibly conditional. Numbers must be supported by claim, track or the source quote, not by a prohibition in limit. Only a genuine meeting-duration invitation in the sole question is exempt. Name proof only when it is the selected row's explicitly external-approved proof; a public source alone does not broaden its claim.
7. Establish the reader, the specific sourced substance and an **approved email voice anchor**: a supplied prior email or a configured approved reference. Record voice_anchor_reference. Missing substance permits an outline or a question, not a ready draft. Missing voice permits a provisional review draft only. Do not collect email history or retain voice samples/corrections without separate authorization. Match the anchor's mechanics, not invented personal details.
8. Draft: open on what the account did/said rather than a date; connect it to the work the reader owns; express the selected persona aim and claim naturally within its limit; finish with one concrete question. Use plain email formatting and the configured greeting/closing. Remove filler, unsupported confidence, generic praise and repetitive contrast. Make a compression pass (aim to cut roughly a third of an initial email where substance survives); the configured final word cap governs.
9. Perform a meaning audit against every source/claim/limit and an anchor-based voice pass. Run `python3 scripts/lint_draft.py --body output/RUN_ID/body.txt`; fix all phrase, punctuation, emoji, hashtag, markdown and word-limit hits. Include a cut list and thin spots resting on inference. Lint cannot determine truth or voice.
10. Build the packet described in scripts/outreach_gate.py, including activity_complete, voice_anchor_reference and optional adoption evidence/review/read-time fields. Run `python3 scripts/outreach_gate.py --packet output/RUN_ID/packet.json`. Exit 1 requires a factual/source-supported correction or stopping; exit 2 requires usable inputs. Preserve reasons and every flag. A boolean or gate marker records an assertion, not independent proof; the reviewer inspects the cited receipts.
11. Propose one Effect with exact To, Subject and full Body, native provider destination/fields, recipient source/title, signal and claim IDs, vertical/persona, selection (bound/vertical/closest), runner-up, relevance, the exact claim.pick_reason, activity coverage, voice reference and gate output. Follow workflows/run.md. Editing any of those approved bytes requires a revised review.
12. Immediately before creation, recheck suppression and live preimages. Create only the approved unsent draft, read it by returned draft ID and compare recipient, subject and full body. Report mismatches/uncertain writes without repair or duplicate creation. After the user actually sends, hand off to signal-followup; a draft alone cannot create a task.

## Outputs and readiness

01_review.md contains the full proposal, reasons, flags, cut list and thin spots. Declare the selected source, receipts, exact body, packet and gate result as artifacts under the reviewed retention contract. Status ready requires every hard check and semantic review preparation above; a warn-mode fit gap stays explicit for the user's decision. At most outreach.max_drafts, and this workflow handles one email per run.

02_result.json records only the approved draft effect, provider ID and complete readback reference. The mail provider owns the draft; no local file establishes that it was sent.

## Human check

Approve the exact recipient and email meaning, claim boundaries, voice, source relevance, the quote-to-claim explanation and any disclosed best_guess/title override. Never send, reply, forward, write CRM records or create tasks here. No approved draft or claim is a reusable authorization for another email.
